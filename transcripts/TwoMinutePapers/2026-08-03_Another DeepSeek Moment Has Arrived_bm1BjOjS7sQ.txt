Build a base, test each part, fix mistakes early, think ahead. Now, we have the same builder, the same toolbox, the same raw knowledge, but now we have a much smarter sequence of actions. And that is why post-training can create a huge leap in performance even when the underlying model stays very similar. Remember this one because you are seeing one of the finest examples of it ever.
